# Debugging CAGE

Debugging CAGE starts by identifying which legality gate failed. Do not treat every rejection as a syntax problem.

Record the exact glyph diagnostic and source location. Then identify whether the issue belongs to formatting, typing, ownership, resources, state, UI, packaging, or another Court. Later Courts may not be reached until an earlier failure is repaired.

Reduce proof complexity when a design creates too many branches, state combinations, ownership joins, or event paths. Repair the evidence and program structure rather than trying to hide the diagnostic.
